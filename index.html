About RISOVO SPORTS

Built for the work behind sport.

RISOVO SPORTS is a sports consultancy focused on the practical, commercial and professional side of sport.

Who we are

Not just advice. Practical support.

Sport is more than what happens during competition. Athletes need professional positioning. Clubs and academies need stronger systems. Events need operational discipline. Sponsors need meaningful opportunities. RISOVO works across these needs.

Our role

We come in to understand the situation, identify what is missing and help build a practical solution. Our work can be a focused project, an audit, a support programme or an ongoing consultancy relationship.

RISOVO does not position itself as a player-agent business. Athlete management is focused on professional support, positioning, social media, digital presence and sponsorship readiness.

Professional PositioningHelp athletes and sporting organisations present themselves with clarity.
Operational ReadinessFind gaps before they become expensive or visible problems.
Commercial ThinkingIdentify assets, opportunities and partnerships that can create value.

Our Approach

Clear thinking.
Practical execution.

We focus on understanding the real problem, building a usable solution and improving it through execution.

How we work

From problem to practical action.

STEP 01

Understand

We start by understanding the athlete, organisation, event or commercial situation. We identify what is working, what is missing and what matters most.

STEP 02

Assess

We break the problem into practical areas — positioning, operations, assets, people, partnerships, processes and priorities.

STEP 03

Build

We create the plan, system, audit, support structure or commercial direction needed for the specific situation.

STEP 04

Execute

Where required, RISOVO supports implementation rather than leaving the client with a document and no next step.

STEP 05

Review

We look at what happened, what changed and what still needs improvement.

STEP 06

Improve

The objective is not a one-time fix. It is to leave behind a stronger way of working.

What this means

Less theory. More useful work.

RISOVO is designed for situations where someone needs to actually solve something: prepare an athlete for sponsorship, make an event operationally ready, map a club's commercial assets or improve an academy's structure.
